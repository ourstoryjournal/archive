7:35 PM

Tomorrow feels heavy.

I've imagined this conversation so many times that I don't even know what to expect anymore. Part of me is terrified that it could be the last time we sit across from each other. Another part keeps holding on to the hope that maybe it isn't too late.

I've spent the last few days looking back at everything, not to find excuses, but to understand where I went wrong. The truth is that I hurt the person I wanted to protect the most. I never woke up intending to break your trust or make you question your place in my life, but intentions don't erase the pain someone feels. I understand that now more than ever.

Guilt is a strange thing. It follows me into every quiet moment. Every time I look at our pictures, I remember a version of us that felt so certain. It makes me wonder how something so beautiful slowly became something so fragile.

I don't know what tomorrow will bring. Maybe we'll talk for hours. Maybe we'll barely say anything. Maybe I'll finally get the chance to tell you everything I've struggled to put into words. Or maybe I'll simply have to accept whatever you choose.

If there's one thing I know with complete certainty, it's that I still love you. Not because I'm afraid of being alone, not because I'm clinging to the past, but because even after everything, when I picture my future, you're still the person standing beside me.

I don't know if life will give us another beginning.

But if it does...

I promise I'll spend every day making sure you never have to question your worth in my life again.

Tonight, all I can do is hope that tomorrow isn't an ending.

I hope it becomes the first page of a new chapter.
