Today

11:48 PM

I spent most of today building this website.

At first, it just felt like another project. HTML, CSS, fixing bugs, moving files around, trying to make everything work. But then came the Gallery. I started going through our photos, one by one.

Some made me smile before I even realized it. Some made me stop and stare for a while. Others brought back little moments I had completely forgotten until I saw them again.

It's strange how a photograph can make you remember things you didn't even know you still remembered. Not just what happened, but how it felt. The conversations. The laughter. The places. The ordinary days that didn't seem important at the time, but somehow became the ones I miss the most.

There were moments I had to stop working because I couldn't look at another picture without feeling overwhelmed.

I don't know what this website will become. Maybe it'll keep growing for years. Maybe this will always be its first page.

All I know is that while I was building it today, I wasn't just sorting through photographs. I was reliving pieces of my life. And for a little while, it felt like I was there again.
